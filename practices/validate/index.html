Validate

Assess whether candidate results are supported for a defined use, scope, and decision context.

Validation is not one test and not a final stamp of truth. It is a structured assessment of whether available evidence supports candidate results for a defined use, population, place, time, scale, and decision context.

Three questions

  1. Was the recorded analysis carried out correctly and completely?
  2. Does the method behave adequately for its intended scientific purpose?
  3. How strong and how limited is the evidence supporting the candidate result?

Verification versus validation

Verification asks whether the recorded analysis was implemented and completed as intended. Confirming that all simulation replicates ran or that a forecast used the intended training period is verification.

Validation asks whether evidence supports using the result for its stated purpose. Evaluating whether a simulation represents relevant biological behavior or whether a forecast performs on later observations is validation.

Both matter, but they answer different questions. Validation does not prove that a model is universally correct or permanently valid.

Validate workflow

  1. Confirm the analysis version and candidate result IDs.
  2. State the intended use and claims being assessed.
  3. Define validation questions and planned evidence.
  4. Verify analysis inputs, execution, and outputs.
  5. Examine assumptions, diagnostics, and model behavior.
  6. Evaluate predictions or forecasts where applicable.
  7. Examine sensitivity, robustness, and uncertainty.
  8. Compare with independent evidence or expected domain behavior.
  9. Record failed, mixed, and inconclusive findings.
  10. Decide whether candidate results support their stated use.

Starting points

Need Guidance
State intended use and plan evidence Validation plan
Confirm analyses and outputs are complete Verify analysis and outputs
Review assumptions and diagnostics Assumptions, diagnostics, and fit
Evaluate a prediction or forecast Prediction and forecast validation
Assess sensitivity and uncertainty Sensitivity, robustness, and uncertainty
Record a validation conclusion Validation decisions and records

Conclusion statuses

Use exactly:

  • not yet evaluated
  • validation in progress
  • supported for stated use
  • supported for stated use with documented limitation
  • evidence inconclusive
  • not supported for stated use
  • superseded

The safe default is not yet evaluated. Do not use generic statuses such as valid, invalid, approved, certified, or scientifically correct.

Validation completion gate

Passing this gate records a scoped conclusion about available evidence. It does not authorize publication, external communication, or operational release.

A scoped validation conclusion may proceed to Collaborate. Inconclusive or not-supported results may still require collaboration to coordinate revision, limitation, discontinuation, or reporting. Validation support does not establish contribution credit or communication readiness.
