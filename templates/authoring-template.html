Metadata convention

Required front matter: title, description, status, practice-level, audience, domains, topics, owner, last-reviewed, and review-cycle.

Recommended values are draft | proposed | approved | retired | template for status; orientation | minimum | recommended | stronger | example for practice level; and annual | semiannual | event-driven for review cycle. Dates use ISO 8601 (YYYY-MM-DD); tags are lowercase and concise. approved requires an explicit lab review decision.
